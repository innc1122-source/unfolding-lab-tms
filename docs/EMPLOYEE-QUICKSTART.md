# 员工快速上手 · Employee Quick Start

## 中文

**1. 登录**：用工号和 HR 发给你的初始密码登录，第一次登录后要改密码。之后可以在右上角头像菜单里「修改密码」。忘了密码点登录页的「忘记密码」，或联系 HR 重置。

**2. 首页**：列出你现在要做的事，比如快到期的课程、待交的自评。

**3. 培训**：左侧「培训」里有「课程」（指派给你的课程）和「发展项目」。课程按模块学完后参加考试；没通过可以按规则补考。

**4. 成长档案**：你的培训记录、绩效结果、荣誉与晋升等成长轨迹。

**5. 绩效评估**：
- **个人绩效**：在「我的评估」提交自评（回顾上一周期目标、设定下一周期目标），与直属上级各自登记 1 对 1 面谈；周期关闭后在「我的绩效」看最终评级，如有异议可在那里申诉。带团队的同事还会看到「团队评估」。
- **组织绩效**：只给一级部门负责人，用于部门之间的匿名互评。

**6. 语言**：右上角「中 / EN」随时切换。手机上同样可用。

有问题请联系 HR。

## English

**1. Sign in** with your employee ID and the initial password HR sends you; you will be asked to change it the first time. Later you can change it from the avatar menu at the top right. Forgot it? Use "Forgot password" on the sign-in page, or ask HR to reset it.

**2. Home** lists what you need to do now, such as courses due soon or a self-review to submit.

**3. Training**: under "Training" on the left you will find "Courses" (assigned to you) and "Development Programs". Finish a course module by module, then take the exam; if you do not pass, you can retake it under the course rules.

**4. Development Record**: your training records, performance results, honours and promotions.

**5. Performance Reviews**:
- **Individual performance**: submit your self-review under "My review" (outcomes against last cycle's goals, goals for the next cycle), and you and your manager each log the 1-on-1. When the cycle closes, see your final rating under "My performance", where you can also file an appeal. People managers also see "Team reviews".
- **Organizational performance**: for heads of top-level departments only — the anonymous peer review between departments.

**6. Language**: switch with "中 / EN" at the top right. Works on your phone too.

Questions? Contact HR.
